System User Guide

Complete guide for CLINTON HARDWARE SYSTEM — hardware, plumbing, welding, roofing & more.

How to Use Each Section

🏠 Dashboard
Overview of your business. Shows total items in stock, today's sales, low stock alerts, and outstanding debts. Charts show 7-day sales trend and top-selling items. Use Export Backup regularly to protect your data.
📦 Stock
All hardware items with category, supplier, unit (pcs, metres, kg, rolls, bags, litres, sheets), buy/sell prices. Red rows = low stock. Add new items or use Stock In to replenish. Totals show stock value and potential profit.
🛒 Sales
Record every sale. Select customer, item, quantity and price. Stock reduces automatically. Edit or delete sales — deleted sales restore stock. After each sale, share the receipt via WhatsApp, download, or print.
🧾 Expenses
Record all running costs — rent, salaries, transport, electricity. Used in profit calculations. Without expenses, your profit will appear higher than it really is.
💳 Credits
Customers with outstanding balances. Tap Pay to record payments. Once fully paid the customer is removed from this list.
🚚 Suppliers
Manage your hardware suppliers. Add name, phone number, and what categories they supply. Each stock item can be linked to a supplier so you know who to call when restocking.
📊 Reports
Daily or Monthly financial statements: Trading Account (Gross Profit), Profit & Loss Account (Net Profit), and Balance Sheet. Print with your business logo or export as text.
⚙️ Settings
Password-protected. Change business name, logo, tagline, username and password. Manage hardware categories and units of measure. Danger Zone lets you reset all data (auto-backup downloaded first).
